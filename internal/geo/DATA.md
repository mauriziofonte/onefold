# Place tables

`cities.tsv.gz` and `countries.tsv` are embedded in the onefold binary and give the `{city}` and `{country}` naming placeholders their values. They are generated by `gen/main.go` and never fetched at run time: no location leaves the computer.

## cities.tsv.gz

One place per line: `name`, ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code, latitude, longitude (degrees, WGS84, 4 decimals), population. Sorted by country, latitude, longitude.

- Source: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org) `cities1000.txt`, the snapshot of 18 March 2020 (the copy published in the `cities-with-1000` 1.0.4 npm package, in the dump format of https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/).
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). GeoNames data is provided "as is", without warranty.
- Changes made by onefold: only populated places (feature class `P`) of at least 5000 people, plus every capital (`PPLC`) and seat of a first-order administrative division (`PPLA`) whatever its size; left out are the sections of a city (`PPLX`), and historical, abandoned, destroyed, religious and farm places (`PPLH`, `PPLQ`, `PPLW`, `PPLCH`, `PPLR`, `PPLF`); only the columns above are kept, coordinates are rounded to 4 decimals. 47,334 places in 245 countries.

## countries.tsv

One country per line: code, English name, Italian name, for the codes that `cities.tsv.gz` uses.

- Source: the region names of the [Unicode CLDR](https://cldr.unicode.org) carried by `golang.org/x/text/language/display`.
- License: [Unicode License v3](https://www.unicode.org/license.txt).

## Regenerating

```sh
go run internal/geo/gen/main.go -cities cities1000.txt -o internal/geo
```

`cities1000.txt` (or `cities500.txt`) is the unzipped file of the GeoNames dump. A newer dump changes some names and adds places: note the date above when you regenerate.
