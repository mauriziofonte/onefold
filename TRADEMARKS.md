# onefold trademark policy

"onefold", the onefold icon and the onefold wordmark (the files in [`assets/brand`](assets/brand)) are trademarks of Maurizio Fonte. This policy says how you may use them. It does not limit what the [license](LICENSE) lets you do with the code: it only covers the name and the logos, which the license does not grant (section 7(e) of the GNU Affero General Public License allows declining trademark rights, and onefold does).

The goal is simple: when someone downloads something called onefold, it must be onefold as published by this project, so that people can trust a tool that touches their only copy of their files.

## Allowed without asking

- Referring to the project by name in articles, reviews, talks, videos, books, courses, forum posts and documentation.
- Saying that your work is "based on onefold", "a fork of onefold", "compatible with onefold" or "works with onefold", as long as it is clear it is not the official project.
- Redistributing the official release binaries, unchanged, under their name.
- Packaging onefold for an operating system or package manager (Linux distributions, Homebrew, Scoop, winget...) under the name onefold, built from an unmodified release of the source, with only the changes packaging needs (paths, build flags, patches that fix the build or a security issue and are offered back to the project).
- Using the logos, unmodified, to link to the project or to show that something is about onefold or works with it.

## Needs written permission

- Distributing a modified version under the name onefold, or with the onefold logos. Forks are welcome: give them another name and another logo, and keep the copyright and license notices the AGPL requires.
- Using "onefold", or a name easily confused with it, in the name of a product, a service, a company, an app store listing or a domain.
- Selling or offering a hosted service called onefold.
- Changing the logos, or combining them with other marks.
- Any use that suggests the project or its author endorses, certifies or is affiliated with you.
